---
title: "CMS meeting backup"
---

The Centre for Music and Science (CMS) at the University of Cambridge uses this app to back up its meeting transcription database to University Google Drive. It encrypts each database snapshot before upload so an authorised service administrator can recover meeting records after a failure. The backup contains transcripts, summaries and meeting metadata. Audio recordings are stored separately and are excluded from these backups.

The app uses Google Drive permission to manage the encrypted files it creates in its backup folder. For details about the information used, retention and how to contact CMS, read the [CMS meeting backup privacy notice](/meeting-backup-privacy/).
